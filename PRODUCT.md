# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Bank fraud analysts and digital banking customers. They need a system that is robust, fast, and highly capable of catching sophisticated fraud.

## Product Purpose
To provide a continuous behavioral authentication platform that builds a unique fingerprint for every user. It clearly shows how the system catches SIM-swap and social engineering attacks via live behavioral drift, providing analysts with actionable insights.

## Brand Personality
Secure, Authoritative, Fast.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS templates, playful/bubbly designs, or overly complex technical dashboards that bury the lead.

## Design Principles
- **Expert Confidence:** The interface must feel rock-solid, reinforcing the security of the underlying AI without overwhelming the user with noise.
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Use live risk gauges and clear attack simulations to instantly communicate behavioral drift and fraud detection.
- **Clarity under Pressure:** For the analyst dashboard, highlight the critical signals immediately to support fast, high-stakes decisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA compliance, with a specific focus on clear, unambiguous contrast for data visualizations and risk scoring so analysts can read signals at a glance.
